Automated Water Dispenser

Developed an automatic water dispenser using Arduino by integrating an ultrasonic sensor to detect water levels, combined with a 20kg torque servo motor. Resolved a problem with a lack of force for the dispensing button with a strap-based mount, using the dispenser's chassis as a mass to counteract the actuation force. Currently planning on expanding by making it more autonomous through ESP32-CAM detection or Raspberry Pi.
